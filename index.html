Fieldwork Clip Poster

Fieldwork Clip Poster is a private tool used by the host of a podcast to share short clips of the show on the podcast's own TikTok account.

Clips are cut from full episodes. The host reviews each clip and approves or skips it. Only approved clips are posted, at scheduled times, with the host's caption.

The app is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's TikTok account.
