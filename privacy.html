Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

What we collect

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • basic profile info (open ID and display name), used only to confirm the right account is connected;
  • an access token, used only to upload and post clips the owner has approved.

We do not collect follower data, messages, contacts, location, or any information about other TikTok users.

How it is stored

The token and profile info are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file that only the owner can read. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until the owner disconnects the app. Disconnecting in TikTok, or asking us, deletes the stored token.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by the podcast's adult host.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
